# Project Overview

## Purpose

This website serves as the digital presence for a competency-based professional development service provider. The platform is designed to:

- Position the company as a comprehensive service provider (beyond traditional training programs)
- Strengthen brand differentiation through a unique competency-based methodology
- Connect with both individual professionals and organizational clients (B2B and B2C)
- Create an empowering, motivating user experience that guides visitors through their professional development journey

## Target Audience

* B2C (Individuals):*
- Early career professionals
- Fresh graduates
- Pre- and post-graduate students

* B2B (Organizations):
- Companies seeking employee development solutions
- NGOs requiring customized training programs
- Startups building their team capabilities

## Unique Value Proposition

Unlike traditional skills-training providers, this platform is built on a standard competency framework  that focuses on holistic competency development rather than isolated skill acquisition. The methodology is fully customizable to meet the specific needs of organizations, groups, and individuals.

## Core Features

* For Individuals:
- 1-on-1 coaching sessions with online booking and payment
- Refund guarantee for service quality assurance
- Progress tracking and gamified learning journey

* For Organizations: 
- Customized program proposals for groups (5+ participants)
- Tailored content development for specific organizational needs
- Choice between general plans and fully customized solutions

* Platform Features:
- Direct chat contact service for immediate support
- Multiple payment options (Cash, Wish, online payments)
- Interactive competency framework visualization
- Gamification elements to encourage engagement
- Mobile-first responsive design

## Design Philosophy

The website balances artistic vision with clarity, creating an experience that feels:
- Empowering 
- Motivating 
- Exciting 
- Trustworthy

The design employs a "journey" metaphor, guiding users step-by-step through their professional development path while avoiding pushy sales tactics, childish themes, or boring corporate vibes.

## Technical Approach

This React-based web application emphasizes:
- Clear, engaging layouts with strong visual storytelling
- Gamification mechanics (progress tracking, interactive elements)
- Prominent methodology showcase (interactive infographic/dedicated section)
- Mobile-first, accessible design
- Integrated payment and communication systems

## Technologies Used

- React (Frontend framework)
- React Router (Navigation)
- JavaScript (ES6+)
- CSS/Styled Components (Styling)
- Vite (Build tool)
- Testing Library + Vitest/Jest (Quality assurance)
